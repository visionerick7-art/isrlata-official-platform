Global Holistic Integrated and Sustainable Transformation Network

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Connecting Local Action with Global Transformation

GHISTN is the international cooperation and knowledge-sharing dimension of ISRLATA. It is designed to connect locally grounded sustainable development initiatives with international institutions, African networks, researchers, communities and development partners.

Its central objective is to connect Kenya's sustainable transformation with global partners and frameworks, while supporting the adaptation and replication of relevant approaches across Africa.

Central Direction

Build bridges between local experience, African cooperation and global knowledge for integrated, ethical and sustainable transformation.

CORE ACTION AREAS

How GHISTN Is Intended to Work

International Partnerships

Develop opportunities for collaboration with relevant international and African institutions, including UNDP, UNEP, FAO, the African Development Bank and the African Union.

African Knowledge Exchange

Encourage knowledge-sharing between African countries on sustainable development, regenerative systems, community action and integrated transformation.

Global Research Cooperation

Support collaborative research, documentation, learning and responsible sharing of practical transformation experiences.

Sustainable Urbanism

Facilitate exchange of ideas and approaches concerning sustainable settlements, urban and rural aesthetics, ecological planning and human wellbeing.

Moral and Cultural Exchange

Promote constructive international dialogue on integrity, cultural identity, community responsibility and ethical development.

Regenerative Transformation

Share approaches to ecological restoration, sustainable resource use, climate resilience and the responsible integration of technology.

PROPOSED GLOBAL STRUCTURE

International Cooperation Framework

ISRLATA Global Secretariat

A proposed international coordination office in Nairobi to support partnerships, communication, documentation and global cooperation.

International Partnership Network

A proposed network connecting institutions, researchers, development organizations, communities and responsible private-sector partners.

Knowledge Exchange Platform

A proposed channel for sharing research, practical lessons, innovation and sustainable development experiences across countries.

EXPECTED CONTRIBUTIONS

Intended Global Impact

  • Stronger international cooperation around sustainable transformation.
  • Knowledge exchange on sustainable urbanism, morality and ecological regeneration.
  • Opportunities to adapt relevant ISRLATA approaches across African contexts.
  • Development of a proposed ISRLATA Global Secretariat in Nairobi.
  • Greater international visibility for African-led transformation research.
  • Positioning Kenya as a potential hub for global holistic transformation research.

Implementation Status

GHISTN is presented here as a proposed component of the ISRLATA framework. International partnerships, the Global Secretariat and knowledge exchange activities require further planning, institutional engagement, resources and implementation.

Part of One Integrated System

GHISTN connects the international dimension of ISRLATA with the eight other core projects.
