Project Overview

About ISULAR

Primary Objective

To establish systems for coordinated utilization of Kenya's local resources — water, soils, minerals, plants, and knowledge — in a sustainable and value-driven manner.

Background and Rationale

Kenya possesses diverse natural, biological, agricultural and knowledge resources. However, resource information, local processing and value-creation opportunities are not always adequately coordinated.

ISULAR proposes an integrated approach to identifying local resources, understanding their availability, promoting responsible utilization and strengthening locally grounded production.

The project connects resource knowledge with environmental responsibility, innovation, employment and sustainable economic development.

Resource Scope

Five Resource Categories

The main resource categories explicitly identified in the ISULAR objective.

01. Water Resources

Understanding local water availability, responsible utilization and sustainable water-resource management.

02. Soil Resources

Recognizing soil characteristics, fertility and sustainable land-use opportunities.

03. Mineral Resources

Identifying local mineral resources and opportunities for responsible, value-driven development.

04. Plant Resources

Exploring locally available plant materials for agriculture, food, biomaterials and other sustainable applications.

05. Knowledge Resources

Recognizing local skills, indigenous knowledge, technical abilities and innovation as valuable development resources.

Proposed Institutional Structure

County Resource Utilization Councils

CRUCs

County Resource Utilization Councils

The ISRLATA framework proposes County Resource Utilization Councils to support coordinated identification, documentation and sustainable utilization of local resources.

Their proposed role is to connect resource information, county stakeholders, local production opportunities and responsible resource management.

Expected Outcomes

Five Intended Results

01. County Resource Inventories

Resource mapping and local inventory creation across all 47 counties.

02. County Coordination

Establishment of County Resource Utilization Councils (CRUCs).

03. Industrial Self-Reliance

Enhanced self-reliance through development of locally available raw materials.

04. Rural Employment

Reduction in rural poverty through resource-based employment opportunities.

05. Local Value Addition

Increased contribution to GDP from locally processed goods.

Responsible Development

Guiding Principles

Sustainable Utilization

Resource use should respect ecological limits and long-term availability.

Local Value Creation

Promote opportunities for responsible local processing and production.

Knowledge Integration

Connect local knowledge, research, technical skills and innovation.

Community Participation

Encourage meaningful participation in resource identification and development.

Long-Term Direction

Locally Rooted Sustainable Development

ISULAR seeks to strengthen Kenya's capacity to understand, utilize and add value to its own resources while maintaining environmental responsibility and long-term sustainability.
