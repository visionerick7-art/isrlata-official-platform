Project Overview

About SURADMA

Primary Objective

To develop aesthetically sustainable and environmentally functional urban and rural spaces that promote tourism, climate resilience, and social well-being.

Background and Rationale

Rapid growth can contribute to urban congestion, inadequate waste management and declining aesthetic and environmental quality in towns and villages.

SURADMA addresses these challenges by proposing the transformation of human settlements into clean, green and inspiring spaces that reflect Kenyan cultural identity while supporting sustainable livelihoods.

The project integrates beauty, ecology and innovation through sustainable design, green infrastructure and community participation.

Project Components

Five Strategic Focus Areas

01. Aesthetic and Green Design

Establishing green parks, urban gardens and landscaped rural centres using indigenous plants and environmentally responsible architecture.

02. Waste Management and Recycling

Promoting decentralized waste collection, recycling, composting and waste-to-art industries.

03. Community Participation

Empowering youth, women and artisans through civic beautification and environmental ownership programmes.

04. Cultural Identity and Tourism

Integrating African art, culture and heritage into public spaces to support ecotourism and creative industries.

05. Governance and Partnerships

Promoting Public–Private–Community Partnerships in design, maintenance and innovation for sustainable development.

Intended Impact

Expected Outcomes

Greener Settlements

Urban and rural areas redesigned with green spaces and effective waste recycling systems.

Environmental Excellence

A long-term ambition for Kenyan towns to become among Africa's cleanest and most beautiful cities.

Green Employment

Creation of employment opportunities in landscaping, design and recycling.

Stronger Partnerships

Strengthened public, private and community partnerships for environmentally responsible urban development.

Climate Resilience

Improved resilience through greening, carbon capture and reduced urban heat.

Long-Term Vision

A Model of Aesthetic Sustainability

To position Kenya as a model of aesthetic sustainability and integrated development, where clean, vibrant and culturally rich environments inspire productivity, attract investment and enhance national pride.
