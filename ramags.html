ISRLATA CORE PROJECT 04

Real-Life African Moral Action and Guider Systems

RAMAGS focuses on practical moral guidance and responsible human action. It seeks to strengthen integrity, dignity, accountability, positive relationships and ethical conduct within individuals, families and communities.

Explore RAMAGS All Projects
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Why RAMAGS matters

Sustainable transformation requires more than physical infrastructure, technology and economic development. It also requires responsible decisions, trustworthy relationships and practical moral responsibility.

Core direction

To develop and promote practical systems of moral guidance that connect values with everyday decisions, personal responsibility, community conduct and sustainable transformation.

ACTION AREAS

RAMAGS areas of focus

The project seeks to make moral responsibility practical, understandable and relevant to real-life situations.

Integrity and Accountability

Encourage honesty, transparency, responsibility and consistency between stated values and actual conduct.

Personal Responsibility

Promote self-discipline, respect for others, responsible choices and awareness of the consequences of individual actions.

Family and Community Relationships

Support respectful relationships, constructive communication, mutual care and peaceful coexistence.

Moral Learning and Guidance

Explore practical learning materials and guidance approaches that help people understand and apply positive values in everyday life.

Conflict Resolution

Encourage dialogue, fairness, reconciliation and responsible approaches to resolving interpersonal and community disagreements.

Ethical Leadership

Promote service, fairness, accountability and responsible decision-making in leadership and institutional relationships.

INTEGRATED GUIDANCE

Connection with the Moral Guider System

RAMAGS connects with the wider ISRLATA Moral Guider System (MGS), which provides a framework for developing moral learning, guidance and responsible community action.

Moral Education

Develop practical educational approaches that connect moral principles with everyday life.

Public Awareness

Explore campaigns and community activities that encourage integrity, respect and responsibility.

Community Guidance

Encourage constructive guidance, mutual support and peaceful approaches to community challenges.

Research and Learning

Support responsible learning and evaluation of moral guidance approaches and their contribution to community wellbeing.
