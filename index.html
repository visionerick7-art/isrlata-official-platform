About ISRLATA

Integrated and Sustainable Real Life African Transformation Action

ISRLATA is a proposed integrated transformation framework originating in Kenya, with a long-term vision of contributing to sustainable development across Africa and the wider world.

Its approach connects real-life challenges and practical action through coordinated projects, research, knowledge systems, community participation, institutional cooperation and long-term sustainability.

Our central approach

Integration, regeneration, dignity and continuity are central principles guiding the ISRLATA framework.

Integrated Action

The Nine Core Projects

The major project areas through which the ISRLATA framework organizes practical transformation.

SURADMA

Sustainable Urban and Rural Aesthetic Development and Management Action

Urban and rural environmental improvement, landscape development, public spaces, aesthetic management and sustainable community environments.

ISULAR

Integrated and Sustainable Utilization of Locally Available Resources

Responsible utilization, value addition and regeneration of locally available resources.

HNIRS

Human–Nature Interaction and Regenerative Systems

The relationship between people and natural systems, environmental responsibility and ecological regeneration.

RAMAGS

Real-Life African Moral Action and Guider Systems

Practical moral guidance, responsible action and community-centred ethical development.

KCHAS

Kenyan Creative Hubs and Adaptation System

Creative hubs, innovation, adaptation and the development of locally relevant solutions.

FTLSS

Farmer–Tenant Leasehold Sustainability System

Sustainable agricultural land-use arrangements and farmer–tenant cooperation.

HuMLIF

Human and Machine Labour Integration Framework

Responsible integration of human capabilities, machinery and technology in productive systems.

AKBOR

African-Kenyan Based Originality Reforms

Originality, African identity, locally grounded innovation and context-sensitive development.

GHISTN

Global Holistic Integrated and Sustainable Transformation Network

A framework for wider cooperation, knowledge exchange and integrated sustainable transformation.

Knowledge and Evidence

Seven Research Streams

Research areas supporting the development, testing and improvement of ISRLATA interventions.

01. Sustainable Resource Utilization and Regeneration

Research into resource efficiency, local materials, regeneration and sustainable production.

02. Climate Resilience and Eco-Agricultural Systems

Research into climate-smart agriculture, food systems, soil, water and ecological resilience.

03. Human-Nature Interaction and Ethical Systems

Research into human wellbeing, environmental relationships and ethical responsibility.

04. Creative Economy and Aesthetic Development

Research into creativity, cultural production, aesthetic environments and livelihoods.

05. Technology and Human–Machine Integration

Research into appropriate technology, automation and responsible human–machine cooperation.

06. Cultural Identity, Moral Evolution, and Heritage Preservation

Research into cultural continuity, heritage, identity and moral development.

07. Global Holistic Integration and Systems Modeling

Research into integrated systems, coordination, measurement and wider transformation models.

Integrated Knowledge Infrastructure

Core ISRLATA Systems

Digital, agricultural and moral guidance systems supporting the wider framework.

DKIP

Digital Knowledge Integration Platform

A proposed digital knowledge environment for information integration, dashboards, learning resources, citizen science and knowledge sharing.

FGB

Farmer Guider Book

A practical agricultural guidance system covering climate-smart farming, local seeds, soil and water management, crop production and economic literacy.

MGS

Moral Guider System

A proposed framework for moral education, public guidance, conflict resolution, responsible action and ethical oversight.

Guiding Philosophy

Philosophical Foundations

The principles and theories that guide the integrated ISRLATA approach.

Law of Perfect Integration

Emphasizes coordination and meaningful relationships between interconnected systems.

Law of Natural Abundance

Focuses on responsible recognition, utilization and regeneration of natural resources.

Theory of Cultural Genetic Renewal

Addresses cultural continuity, identity and the renewal of valuable heritage.

Principle of Planetary Responsibility

Promotes responsibility toward the environment and future generations.

Integrated Transformation Theory

Organizes transformation around unity, regeneration, dignity and continuity.

Law of Regenerative Equilibrium

Emphasizes balanced relationships between human activity and regenerative systems.

Long-Term Development

Six-Year Implementation Framework

A phased approach proposed in the ISRLATA framework.

Phase 1 — Year 1

Foundation and institutional preparation.

Phase 2 — Year 2

Pilot integration across six counties.

Phase 3 — Years 3–4

National rollout and expansion.

Phase 4 — Year 5

Consolidation and innovation.

Phase 5 — Year 6

Sustainability and transition.

Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning

The proposed framework includes annual reporting, county scorecards, public impact dashboards, external audits and measurable sustainability indicators.
