ISRLATA CORE PROJECT 05

Kenyan Creative Hubs and Adaptation System

KCHAS focuses on creative development, practical innovation, skills, cultural expression and locally adapted solutions. It seeks to connect Kenyan creativity with real-life community needs and sustainable opportunities.

Explore KCHAS All Projects
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Creativity connected to real life

Creative potential exists in communities, institutions, local industries and individuals. KCHAS seeks to create pathways through which this potential can be developed, adapted and applied to practical challenges.

Core direction

To encourage creative hubs and adaptation systems that support skills, innovation, cultural identity, enterprise development and locally relevant solutions within Kenya.

ACTION AREAS

KCHAS areas of focus

The project connects creative expression with practical learning, production and community development.

Community Creative Hubs

Explore spaces where young people, artists, makers and community members can collaborate, learn and develop practical ideas.

Skills and Talent Development

Support practical skills development, creative problem-solving, mentorship and opportunities for learning by doing.

Culture and Heritage

Encourage preservation and creative expression of Kenyan languages, traditions, knowledge, arts and cultural identity.

Innovation and Adaptation

Encourage solutions adapted to local resources, environmental conditions, livelihoods and community needs.

Creative Enterprise

Explore pathways for turning original ideas, artistic work, local products and practical innovations into sustainable enterprises.

Digital Creativity

Explore digital content, animation, music, design, media and technology as tools for creative participation and opportunity.

ADAPTATION AND APPLICATION

From creative ideas to practical solutions

Local Materials

Encourage exploration of locally available materials and resources for useful products, design and production.

Community Challenges

Connect creative activities with practical challenges in agriculture, environment, infrastructure and everyday living.

Collaboration

Encourage cooperation between communities, educational institutions, creative professionals, enterprises and other relevant partners.

Learning and Improvement

Support testing, feedback, adaptation and improvement of creative ideas before wider application.
