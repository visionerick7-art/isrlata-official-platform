ISRLATA CORE PROJECT 08

African-Kenyan Based Originality Reforms

AKBOR focuses on strengthening originality in African and Kenyan development. It explores how cultural identity, indigenous knowledge, local creativity, locally available resources and practical innovation can contribute to sustainable transformation.

Explore AKBOR All Projects
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Originality as a foundation for transformation

Sustainable development should recognise the value of local knowledge, cultural identity, creativity and the conditions in which communities actually live. AKBOR explores approaches that strengthen originality while remaining open to useful knowledge and innovation from other parts of the world.

Core direction

To promote African-Kenyan originality through the recognition, preservation, development and practical application of local knowledge, cultural resources, creative capacity and context-appropriate innovation.

ACTION AREAS

AKBOR areas of focus

The project connects cultural identity and originality with practical development, learning and production.

African Cultural Identity

Encourage appreciation and responsible expression of African cultural identities, histories, languages and community knowledge.

Indigenous and Local Knowledge

Recognise and explore relevant indigenous knowledge in agriculture, ecology, food systems, craftsmanship and community life, with respect for its holders.

Original Products and Designs

Encourage the development of original Kenyan and African products, designs, technologies and creative solutions.

Language and Heritage

Support the preservation, use and intergenerational transmission of African languages, oral traditions, stories and cultural heritage.

Local Resource Innovation

Explore responsible ways of applying locally available resources and materials in products, enterprises and sustainable production.

Context-Appropriate Reforms

Encourage development approaches that respond to African and Kenyan social, environmental, economic and cultural realities.

PRACTICAL APPLICATION

Where originality can be strengthened

Agriculture and Food

Explore local crops, seeds, food knowledge, agricultural practices and appropriate innovations that support sustainable food systems.

Arts and Creative Industries

Encourage original music, visual arts, animation, design, storytelling, fashion and other creative expressions.

Local Manufacturing

Promote exploration of locally designed products, material innovation, craftsmanship and context-appropriate manufacturing.

Education and Knowledge

Encourage learning systems that recognise local contexts, practical knowledge, originality and critical thinking.

Community Development

Support locally informed approaches to environmental care, livelihoods, social wellbeing and development.

Digital and Global Participation

Explore digital platforms that help African creators and innovators document, share and present original work globally.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

How AKBOR approaches originality

Cultural Respect

Treat cultural knowledge and heritage with respect, including the rights and contributions of the communities that preserve them.

Authenticity

Encourage original work, honest representation and recognition of the sources of ideas and cultural expressions.

Responsible Adaptation

Adapt useful external knowledge and technology to local conditions without abandoning valuable local identity and knowledge.

Intergenerational Continuity

Support the transmission and development of useful cultural and practical knowledge across generations.

EXPECTED CONTRIBUTIONS

03

04

Greater cultural continuity and global participation
