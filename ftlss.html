ISRLATA CORE PROJECT 06

Farmer–Tenant Leasehold Sustainability System

FTLSS focuses on sustainable arrangements between farmers and landowners. It seeks to connect access to agricultural land with fair agreements, productive farming, responsible resource management and long-term land sustainability.

Explore FTLSS All Projects
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Connecting land access with sustainable agriculture

Access to suitable agricultural land can create opportunities for farmers and landowners. However, unclear agreements, insecure access and poor land management can undermine productivity and long-term sustainability.

Core direction

To explore sustainable farmer–tenant and landowner arrangements that encourage responsible land use, mutually understood agreements, agricultural productivity and the protection of land resources.

ACTION AREAS

FTLSS areas of focus

The project seeks to address both the human and environmental dimensions of agricultural land use.

Land Access Opportunities

Explore arrangements that connect farmers who need productive land with landowners willing to make land available under agreed terms.

Fair Lease Arrangements

Encourage clear agreements covering lease duration, payments, responsibilities, permitted activities and conditions for renewal or termination.

Productive Land Utilization

Promote suitable agricultural practices, land-use planning and responsible utilization according to local conditions.

Soil and Water Conservation

Encourage soil protection, responsible irrigation, water conservation and practices that maintain the productive capacity of land.

Farmer and Landowner Cooperation

Support communication, mutual understanding, transparency and cooperation between participating farmers and landowners.

Long-Term Land Sustainability

Encourage arrangements that protect land quality, reduce degradation and consider the interests of future land users.

PRACTICAL FRAMEWORK

How a sustainable arrangement can work

FTLSS can explore a structured process that makes land access and agricultural responsibilities easier to understand and manage.

01. Land Identification

Identify available land, its condition, access, water resources and suitable agricultural uses.

02. Farmer and Landowner Matching

Establish the needs and expectations of participating farmers and landowners.

03. Agreement and Responsibilities

Document mutually agreed terms, duration, payment arrangements, maintenance duties and land-use conditions.

04. Sustainable Production

Support productive farming while applying responsible soil, water and environmental management practices.

05. Monitoring and Review

Review agricultural performance, compliance with agreements, land condition and opportunities for improvement.

06. Renewal and Continuity

Explore appropriate renewal, transition or closure arrangements that respect agreed rights and responsibilities.

EXPECTED CONTRIBUTIONS

What FTLSS seeks to support

01

Improved opportunities for responsible agricultural land access

02

Clearer and more cooperative farmer–landowner relationships

03

More productive and sustainable land utilization

04

Protection of soil, water and long-term land productivity
