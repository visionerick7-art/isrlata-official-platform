ISRLATA CORE PROJECT 03

Human–Nature Interaction and Regenerative Systems

HNIRS promotes healthier relationships between people, ecosystems and the natural resources that sustain life. It focuses on regeneration, ecological responsibility and practical human participation in environmental wellbeing.

Explore HNIRS All Projects
PROJECT OVERVIEW

What HNIRS addresses

Human wellbeing and environmental wellbeing are interconnected. HNIRS approaches nature not merely as a source of materials, but as a living system requiring care, balance and regeneration.

Core direction

To encourage practical systems in which human activities protect, restore and sustainably interact with natural ecosystems while supporting livelihoods and quality of life.

ACTION AREAS

HNIRS areas of focus

The project connects environmental protection with practical community activities and sustainable development.

Ecological Regeneration

Support restoration of degraded environments, vegetation recovery, biodiversity and healthier relationships between people and ecosystems.

Water and Soil Stewardship

Promote responsible water use, soil conservation, erosion reduction and practices that maintain the natural foundations of production.

Human–Nature Interaction

Encourage meaningful everyday interaction with nature through learning, outdoor activities, community participation and environmental care.

Biodiversity and Habitat

Encourage protection of indigenous species, habitat improvement, ecological connectivity and responsible use of biological resources.

Regenerative Livelihoods

Explore livelihood opportunities that can support communities while maintaining or improving the natural resource base.

Environmental Learning

Strengthen practical environmental awareness, ecological knowledge and community responsibility across generations.

INTEGRATED APPROACH

How HNIRS connects with ISRLATA

Agriculture

Connect regenerative land management, soil health, water conservation and climate-resilient production.

Community Wellbeing

Promote safe, healthy and attractive environments that support human dignity and everyday wellbeing.

Technology and Innovation

Explore appropriate technologies for environmental observation, resource management and regenerative solutions.

Culture and Responsibility

Recognise the value of indigenous knowledge, responsible stewardship and intergenerational environmental care.
