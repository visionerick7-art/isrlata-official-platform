ISRLATA CORE PROJECT 07

Human and Machine Labour Integration Framework

HuMLIF explores practical integration between human labour, machinery and technology. It seeks to improve productivity and working conditions while recognising human dignity, skills, creativity and the importance of sustainable employment.

Explore HuMLIF All Projects
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Human capability working with technology

Machinery and technology can extend human capabilities, improve efficiency and reduce difficult or repetitive work. Their application must also consider employment, safety, accessibility, skills and the distribution of economic benefits.

Core direction

To explore balanced systems in which human knowledge, labour and decision-making are integrated with appropriate machinery and technology for productive, dignified and sustainable development.

ACTION AREAS

HuMLIF areas of focus

The framework considers the roles of people, machines and institutions in productive systems.

Human–Machine Cooperation

Explore working arrangements where people and machines complement one another according to their respective capabilities.

Appropriate Technology

Encourage the selection of affordable, maintainable and context-appropriate technologies that respond to real community and industry needs.

Skills and Capacity Development

Support practical training in machinery operation, maintenance, digital tools, technical skills and responsible technology use.

Productivity and Work Design

Explore ways to improve production processes, reduce unnecessary effort and organise work more effectively.

Human Dignity and Employment

Consider worker safety, fair opportunities, decent working conditions and the effects of technological change on livelihoods.

Maintenance and Resource Efficiency

Encourage repair, maintenance, efficient energy use and responsible management of equipment and materials.

PRACTICAL APPLICATIONS

Where HuMLIF can be applied

Agriculture

Explore appropriate machinery for land preparation, planting, irrigation, harvesting, processing and post-harvest handling.

Manufacturing

Connect human craftsmanship and technical knowledge with tools, machinery and production systems.

Waste and Environmental Management

Explore machinery-assisted waste collection, sorting, recycling and resource recovery while retaining essential human oversight.

Construction and Infrastructure

Consider how appropriate equipment and skilled human labour can support safer, more efficient construction activities.

Digital and Knowledge Work

Explore responsible use of digital systems, data tools and automation to support human learning and decision-making.

Community Enterprises

Support small enterprises and community production systems in accessing suitable equipment, skills and technical support.

INTEGRATION PRINCIPLES

How HuMLIF approaches technology

Human-Centred Design

Technology should respond to human needs, safety, dignity and practical working conditions.

Complementary Capabilities

Assign tasks according to the strengths and limitations of people and machines.

Accessibility and Affordability

Consider whether technologies are accessible, affordable and maintainable in their intended communities and working environments.

Responsible Innovation

Assess environmental effects, safety, employment implications and long-term resource requirements.
