ISRLATA CENTRAL DIGITAL SYSTEM

Digital Knowledge Integration Platform

A central digital knowledge environment designed to connect research, practical action, project information, data, learning and innovation across ISRLATA.

Knowledge is not meant to remain stored. It should be organized, shared, understood and transformed into responsible real-life action.

Platform Architecture

Four connected knowledge functions form the initial foundation of the DKIP.

01. Knowledge Repository

A planned digital library for ISRLATA research reports, technical manuals, training guides, project documents and cultural archives.

Foundation module

02. Data Dashboards

A future data visualization environment for agriculture, climate, environmental conditions and socio-economic indicators.

Foundation module

03. AI Learning Modules

A planned learning and analytical environment supporting predictive insights on food security, land use, climate impacts and integrated sustainability.

Future development

04. Citizen Science Tools

A planned mobile-based contribution system through which communities can submit environmental and cultural observations for responsible analysis.
